FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Straight answers before you book. Still not covered? Send us a message or text (914) 386-4984.

Q01 Do I need to provide water or power?

We just need access to a standard electrical outlet to power our equipment — no need for an outdoor water spigot on your end.

Q02 How long does a typical detail take?

Services typically take about 1 to 1.5 hours for a specific job like Interior or Exterior detailing, or roughly 2 to 3+ hours for a Full Detail.

Q03 What if it rains on my appointment day?

We'll be happy to reschedule your appointment for a more convenient time.

Q04 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, Zelle, Venmo, and all major Credit/Debit cards.

Q05 Should I do anything before you arrive?

We kindly ask that clients remove all personal belongings and valuables from the vehicle prior to our arrival.

Q06 Do you only service homes, or other locations too?

We can perform the service wherever it's most convenient for you — home, apartment, office, or commercial lot — provided there's adequate space and access.
